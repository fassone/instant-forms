# Sample Legal Content

This folder owns the Spanish layout-test documents and their shared link labels/paths. The public route registry mounts them as standalone `legal_page` actions; product templates opt into their links through `page.legalLinks`.

These documents are explicitly unapproved samples. Keep the prominent sample notice, unconfirmed company/contact details and draft-only indexing restrictions until reviewed content is supplied. Do not invent legal commitments, contact channels, retention periods or compliance claims.

Document sections use escaped Markdown, not raw HTML. Layout, safe return URLs and browser behavior belong to the platform rendering/routing layers. Changes should preserve unique section anchors and pass the legal-page unit and browser tests.
